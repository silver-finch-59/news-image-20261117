TUESDAY, 17 NOVEMBER 2026
Five stories that defined the day — from the Kerch Strait to the Strait of Hormuz.
WAR IN UKRAINE — LEAD STORY

Kerch Bridge Falls

Ukraine’s second-night strike drops a road arch and a span of the 19-kilometre bridge to Crimea into the strait. Moscow halts all traffic as Russia pounds Kyiv’s bridges in reply.

Kyiv says a seven-stage operation stripped the bridge’s air defences first. Rail traffic resumed after a pause; the road link is out of action.

02GULF CRISIS

Fire in the Strait

Tankers burn in the Strait of Hormuz after mine and missile strikes, as a third US carrier, USS Theodore Roosevelt, arrives in the region. US intelligence says Iran drew up plans for a dirty-bomb or chemical attack in Washington.

03DIPLOMACY

Three Leaders, One Table

Xi, Trump and Putin will meet in Shenzhen on Thursday — the first-ever trilateral summit of the three nuclear powers, on the sidelines of the APEC leaders’ meeting.

04WASHINGTON

The $4.8 Trillion Question

The Pentagon fails its financial audit for the ninth year in a row. Auditors could not verify more than $4.8 trillion in assets — the only major US agency never to pass.

05TECH & MARKETS

Nvidia’s $116 Billion Day

Record quarterly revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103%, on the day its board created a national-security committee — and ruled out Jensen Huang’s return as chief executive.

ALSO TODAY

Trump lands in Manila for Quad talks

The US president opens a three-day Asia tour with Japan, Australia and India, before flying to Shenzhen for APEC.
